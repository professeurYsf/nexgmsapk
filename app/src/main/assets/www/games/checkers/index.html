How To Play

  • Move your pieces diagonally, forward only, one square at a time.
  • Capturing is mandatory — if a capture is available, you must take it.
  • If several captures are possible, you must play the one that captures the most pieces.
  • Reach the last row to crown a king — kings move and capture any distance along the diagonal.
