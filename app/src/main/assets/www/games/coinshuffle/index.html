Coin Shuffle Memory

Follow the coin. Trust your memory.

Game mode

4 rounds, harder each time: Easy, Medium, Hard, Very Hard.

Best –

4 stages, getting harder each time. P1 follows the red coin, P2 the blue one.

Tip: rotate your phone to landscape for a bigger board.

Game Paused

How To Play

  • Memorise where the special coin is: red for you (and blue for Player 2).
  • All coins flip face down and swap places.
  • When they stop you have 5 seconds to pick the coin you followed. No pick = stage lost.
  • 1 player: 4 coins, 4 rounds: Easy, Medium, Hard, Very Hard.
  • 2 players: 5 coins, 4 stages. Each player picks on their own side (P1 bottom, P2 top). You may pick the same coin.
