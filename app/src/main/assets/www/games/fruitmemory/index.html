How To Play

  • 12 fruits are shown in 12 circles for 12 seconds. Memorise their places!
  • Then they disappear and appear on both sides of the board.
  • Drag each fruit into the circle where it was.
  • Right place: you score a point and keep playing. Wrong place: it counts as a mistake (and in 2 players, your turn ends).
  • Place all 12 fruits to finish. Most points wins!
