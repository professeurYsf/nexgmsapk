How To Play

  • Flip two cards per turn to find a matching pair.
  • If they match, you score a pair and play again.
  • If they don't match, the cards flip back and the turn changes.
  • Play against the AI, or 2 players on the same device.
  • Whoever finds the most pairs wins!
